One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened today.

## The line (129 characters, verified by local run)

> At 6 a.m. the neighbour's cat, who hates everyone, chose my lap for the first time — and stayed through two whole cups of coffee.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes exactly the line above to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), a single line with no trailing newline.

## Why this line

Warmth first: a creature that trusts nobody chose a lap. Surprise: it stayed. One concrete detail anchors it — 6 a.m., two cups of coffee — and the wording is plain, not greeting-card.
